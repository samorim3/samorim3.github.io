Project Background & Technical Architecture

1. From 2025 Lessons to Automation in 2026

In my 2025 Annual Investment Review, one key takeaway emerged: actively tracking 19 individual companies across multiple markets is practically impossible to sustain manually. As a Dividend Growth Investor (DGI), my objective is long-term dividend sustainability, strong free cash flow coverage, and avoiding value traps.

To turn those learnings into an operational edge, I built this platform to address two daily operational challenges:

  • A Rapid Pre-Screening Filter: In-depth balance sheet and cash flow reviews take hours. I needed a rapid first-stage screener to evaluate candidate businesses in seconds, verifying whether a company justifies deeper analysis before allocating hours of manual research.
  • Automated Buy & Sell Discipline: Quality companies rarely trade at attractive valuations, while existing positions can become overextended. Rather than constantly checking broker apps, I built an automated watcher that tracks target buy prices (margin of safety) and target sell prices (profit-taking or rebalancing).

📊 Institutional-Grade DGI Screener

  • 5-Pillar Score (0–100): Yield, FCF Coverage, Solvency (Net Debt/EBITDA < 3x), Operational Growth (Revenue/Net Income CAGR), and the Chowder Rule.
  • Ordinary vs. Extraordinary Dividends: One-off special dividends are isolated from core metrics (yield, payout, 5Y CAGR), preventing cyclical windfalls from masquerading as aristocrats.
  • Quality Multiples: Integrates Return on Invested Capital (ROIC > 10%), Operating Margins (> 12%), and 5-Year Share Count Evolution (rewarding buybacks vs. dilution).

📱 Real-Time Watchlist & Telegram Alerts

  • Dynamic Monitoring: Real-time price tracking across global exchanges (US, Euronext Lisbon, Paris, Frankfurt, etc.).
  • One-Click Scorecard Dispatch: Sends structured executive summaries directly to a personal Telegram channel or chat.
  • Automated Trigger Alerts: Background threshold checks immediately notify when market dips hit target buy prices or rallies hit exit targets.
