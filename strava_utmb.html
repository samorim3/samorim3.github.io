STRAVA Analysis
to get UTMB done

Endurance data analytics, physiological load modeling, and pacing strategy for the Ultra-Trail du Mont-Blanc.

🚧

Project Under Active Development

This project is currently in active progress. The analytics engine is processing activity streams from the Strava API, integrating GPS elevation profiles, Grade Adjusted Pace (GAP), and physiological training load algorithms. Interactive visualizations and race simulation models will be published soon.

Project Overview

The Ultra-Trail du Mont-Blanc (UTMB) is widely recognized as one of the pinnacle ultra-endurance mountain races in the world, covering approximately 171 kilometers (106 miles) with over 10,000 meters (32,800 feet) of positive elevation gain through France, Italy, and Switzerland.

Successfully crossing the finish line in Chamonix requires a disciplined balance between raw cardiovascular conditioning, alpine hill climbing efficiency, fatigue resistance, and race-day nutrition. This project leverages granular telemetry from Strava activities to model training progression and build a predictive strategy for the race.

Key Modules Under Development

🛰️ Strava API Data Pipeline

Automated ingestion and cleansing of raw GPX/FIT telemetry, including second-by-second timestamps, GPS coordinates, heart rate zones, cadence, and barometric elevation.

📈 Training Load & Fatigue (TRIMP/TSS)

Quantifying Acute Training Load (ATL), Chronic Training Load (CTL), and Training Stress Balance (TSB) to peak fitness while minimizing the risk of overtraining injuries.

⛰️ Vertical Ascent (VAM) & Grade Pacing

Modeling climbing velocity (VAM in m/h) and Grade Adjusted Pace (GAP) to assess power output on steep alpine ascents and downhill quad impact tolerance.

🗺️ UTMB Race Pacing & Cut-Off Simulator

Simulating sector-by-sector checkpoint splits (Les Contamines, Courmayeur, Grand Col Ferret, Champex-Lac) based on historical fatigue curves and altitude profiles.
